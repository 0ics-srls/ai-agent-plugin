# Code of conduct

This project is a place to make a set of skills better. Everyone who opens an issue, proposes a
change or comments is expected to keep to this page, and so is the maintainer.

## What is expected

- Talk about the text and what the agent did with it, not about the person who wrote it.
- Be precise: say what you saw, what you expected, and how to see it again.
- Assume the other person is in good faith and has a reason; ask for it before judging it.
- Accept a "no" with its reason. A proposal that is declined can be argued once, with new facts.
- Write so that someone reading in their second language understands you.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, and attacks on a person or a group, for whatever reason.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Persistently derailing a discussion after being asked to stop.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove a comment, close or lock a discussion, and block an account from
the repository, with or without a warning depending on how serious it is. A decision can be
questioned once, privately, through the contact below.

## Reporting

Report a problem of conduct privately to the maintainer, at mail@0ics.it. Reports are kept
confidential.
